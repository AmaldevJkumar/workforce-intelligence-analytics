## Root Cause Analysis

### Top 3 Attrition Drivers (Model Insights)

**1. Below-Market Compensation (Weight: 32%)**
- 68% of recent voluntary departures cited compensation
- 47% of high-risk employees are paid below market (-5% or more)
- Engineering gap: avg 8% below market
- Sales gap: avg 6% below market

**Recommendation**: Immediate market adjustment budget allocation of $2.4M

**2. Burnout / Excessive Overtime (Weight: 24%)**
- 156 employees averaging > 40 overtime hours/month
- Correlation: 45% higher attrition in high-OT employees
- Concentrated in Engineering (89 employees) and Operations (43 employees)

**Recommendation**: 
- Hiring freeze lift: add 15 FTEs in Engineering, 8 in Operations
- Mandate: No sustained overtime > 40 hrs/month

**3. Career Stagnation (Weight: 19%)**
- 212 employees overdue for promotion (> 24 months)
- 34% of high-risk have not been promoted in 30+ months
- Particularly acute in individual contributor tracks

**Recommendation**: 
- Accelerate promotion cycle for Q1
- Create senior IC career paths (avoid management-only advancement)
